# Wine Journal — Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 8, 2026

Wine Journal is a personal-use app for tracking wine tastings and cellar inventory.

## Account Information
When you sign in, we collect your email address via Supabase Authentication to create and secure your account, used solely to identify you and sync your data across devices.

## Content You Create
Tasting notes, ratings, cellar entries, and photos you add are stored so they're available across sessions and devices. This content is private to your account and is never shared with other users or made public.

## Bottle Photo Identification
When you photograph a bottle, you can optionally search for a matching bottle image instead of using your own photo. This sends the wine's name/vintage or your photo to Google Cloud Vision and Google Images (via SerpApi) to find candidate images. If you select one, it is downloaded and stored with your entry. We do not control or verify usage rights for images returned by these services — this feature is provided for personal convenience only.

## What We Don't Do
- No analytics or advertising SDKs.
- We do not sell or share your data for marketing purposes.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Data Storage
Your data is stored via Supabase's cloud infrastructure and cached locally on your device for offline access.
